# NATO and historical invasions

Reviewed 12 October 2026. Channel: `#view=politics&topic=nato-and-invasions`.

The user supplied `nato_defensive.png`. Its author, publication date and underlying citations are unspecified. The original image remains outside the repository. The derived data transcribes its 62 actor/date claims for examination, not endorsement.

## Counting and attribution

- 61 entries precede NATO's founding treaty of 4 April 1949.
- Fourteen rows refer to participants in the 1812 campaign, not fourteen independent invasions.
- Century counts are 3, 11, 5, 2, 18, 22 and 1 for the 1300s, 1500s, 1600s, 1700s, 1800s, 1900s and 2000s.
- Historical claims remain unverified at the individual campaign/contingent level. A valid chronological observation does not validate the image's labels.
- The modern-border map is omitted: it collapses historical polities, coalition contingents, volunteers, the USSR and present Russia into misleadingly stable identities.
- The modern claim of NATO-commanded bombing is unsupported by the image. The page distinguishes Ukrainian strikes, equipment/training support and an operation under NATO command. NATO's own legal position is attributed to NATO.

The second chart covers six selected documented NATO operations or support missions. This is not a census or an all-defensive characterization. Bar endpoints are year-level summaries; short operations use minimum-width markers and ongoing support ends at the review snapshot. Operation dialogs provide periods, mandates, limitations and direct sources.

Sources include the North Atlantic Treaty, NATO's operation records and 8 October Ukraine relationship page, UN Resolution 1973, Fondation Napoléon's campaign timeline and CSIS's July 2026 analysis. Links are stored with the data and rendered alongside the claims they support. Sources establish their specific records, not blanket legal approval of all actions.
