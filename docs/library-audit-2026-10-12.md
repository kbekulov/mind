## Limits carried forward

The wars-by-duration and wars-by-outcome collections remain attributed reference datasets (127 and 55 entries). Their arithmetic, dates, filtering and presentation were reviewed; this audit does not independently verify every classification or transform their selected samples into a census of wars. Definitions and unresolved source limitations remain visible.

The NATO image's 61 historical entries still require campaign-by-campaign verification. The page's counts refer to image rows, not verified invasions.

Job checks are a maintenance pass one day after the discovery run. No country search is newly marked complete. The prior country/source inventories and all five role-family discovery gaps are carried into `data/search-runs.json`. Safran and Cronos checks were blocked; this is uncertainty, not proof of closure. Daycoval's visible Apply button conflicts with its frozen vacancy state; WNS has an application control but lacks responsibilities and qualifications. Both remain unpublished as active vacancies.

The fertility, parliamentary, casualty and exchange sources use different reporting calendars. A page checked in October does not make an August, June or 2024 observation an October measurement. Source links are published beside each chart or finding; inaccessible or unresolved evidence is not silently promoted to verified status.
