# Dating is a mess. Here’s the only way to fix it

## What's going on with dating?

Cambridge confirmed: 50% of single men in America have given up trying to meet someone. But it's not just America - it's happening all over the world. In this video, I'll break down some startling data about the state of dating today and explain why we're in this situation.

Yes, nowadays women often make unrealistic demands on the men they are willing to date. But I've helped thousands of men improve their dating, relationships, and sex lives, and I can say: it's not just that. I will also tell you about the only way to get out of this crisis.

And most importantly, about how you can still achieve success in dating, even if the situation gets even worse. And this will most likely happen. But first, let's look at the data. According to a 2025 study published by Cambridge, 42% of Americans are not in a relationship.

Among single men, 50% have simply given up on everything. And among single women there are as many as 65%. Due to this epidemic of loneliness, approximately 34% of the US population is diagnosed with mental disorders, including depression and anxiety disorders. And because of this, the very thought of dating seems even more hopeless.

This also explains why more and more men are turning to pornography, OnlyFans - a paid subscription service, including erotic materials - and virtual interlocutors based on artificial intelligence. But that's not even the craziest thing. Look at the graph, which was also published in a recent Cambridge article.

It shows how many sexual partners modern men have throughout their lives. As you can see, the one percent of men with the most partners have at least 287. The top ten percent have about 38.

The top twenty percent have at least 21 partners. The remaining eighty percent of men have between one and seven sexual partners over their lifetimes. The pie chart on the right shows that the top one percent of men account for 20% of all sex.

The top five percent account for 24% of all sex. That is, almost half of all sex goes to five percent of men. And the bottom thirty percent of men account for only 3%.

Another study, about mutual liking on the dating app Tinder, shows a similar picture. The bottom eighty percent of men compete for about the bottom twenty-two percent of women in such applications. And the top twenty percent of men get the top seventy-eight percent of women. In other words, most modern men are practically invisible to the average woman.

They find it difficult to even attract female attention, not to mention relationships. Why is this happening? First you need to understand the concept of “hypergamy” - then a lot will become clearer. For those who don’t know: hypergamy is the desire inherent in women to choose a certain kind of partner.

## Why does the author talk about hypergamy?

To put it simply, women usually want to date or sleep with a person whose status is higher than their own. “Status” here must be understood very broadly. Therefore, they tend to prefer a man who is taller, stronger, earns more, has more charisma, is more influential, has more useful connections and more power than themselves.

This is why women are drawn to the twenty percent of men they find most attractive, as we saw in the data. Hypergamy is a well-described strategy for choosing a partner, and every woman has this instinct. What's more, it's a good thing that it exists: throughout history, it has motivated men to develop abilities, accumulate wealth, increase their status, and contribute to society in order to become more attractive to a potential mate.

It encouraged competition between men and gave us ambition. Yes, it helped drive human civilization forward, damn it! So hypergamy was, and still is, enormously beneficial.

But nature kept it within certain limits. In the days of the cavemen, a woman needed protection and provision from a man for her very survival. Therefore, she had to choose from those who were nearby: otherwise she could die. Later, as society developed, we began to establish social norms such as monogamous marriage, that is, the union of one man and one woman.

In many countries it has become prohibited to have multiple wives. For example, ancient Germanic tribes even executed people for adultery. So such orders have existed for quite a long time.

We did this for the further development of society. After all, monogamy, which was supported by social norms, meant that every man would have a woman, and every woman would have a man who would protect her. Most men were given the opportunity to have sex and have a family to take care of. They developed a personal interest in the well-being of society. This, let's say, kept the very bottom thirty percent of men who don't get sex from violent rebellion. Therefore, marriage in a sense was a system of equalizing opportunities for men - or, if you like, for all civilization. In modern American terminology, this would be called DEI: diversity, equity and inclusion programs.

Marriage helped curb hypergamy. Thanks to this, all the wonderful technological achievements that you see around you arose.

## The turning point of the 1960s: contraception and economic independence

But the dating crisis began to gain momentum in the 1960s - during the sexual revolution and the rise of feminism. Contraception appeared, and casual sex began to be perceived as the norm. Monogamy went to hell, but promiscuity went sharply up: now women could share the most attractive man without the immediate consequence of pregnancy.

They no longer had to fear either social condemnation or accidental pregnancy. In addition, women began to go to work in large numbers. From 1950 to 2000 the proportion of women participating in working life doubled.

Before 1950, only a third of women had jobs. In addition, women began to earn more. From 1980 to 2013, the average annual earnings of American women, adjusted for inflation, increased by 30%: from 30 thousand to 39 thousand dollars. The average earnings of men remained at 50 thousand dollars.

Not to mention, most U.S. companies have instituted DEI programs and other measures that encourage women's employment and advancement. But here comes the problem: according to the principle of hypergamy, women want a man who is in a better position than themselves. They want to choose a partner “higher than themselves.”

But throughout the history of civilization, a man most often found a wife because he was considered economically attractive - someone who was able to support a family. Now many men have lost this position. It is increasingly difficult for a man with the most ordinary, average job to attract a woman and find a companion.

And this is not just my opinion. That same Cambridge article directly states: the more women earn, the higher their demands on men become. Therefore, after the feminist movement of the 1960s, the birth rate literally fell off a cliff.

And since then the situation has not improved. And here is another study - more than two hundred thousand people in Sweden, which is considered perhaps the most equal country in the world. It showed that women still chose men who earned more than them, even if they were less educated. Therefore, if you want to succeed in dating, you need to either earn more or develop other qualities that will make you stand out from the rest and make you more attractive.

I will return to this later. But this is exactly how the current dating crisis began: with women freed from the risk of pregnancy and embraced by the sexual revolution.

Then women's demands began to grow. Women began to share the most attractive men among themselves because casual relationships no longer had immediate consequences. But the situation was not yet so difficult: women could choose mainly among men from their area, their immediate environment, their local community. The circle within which they could look for a “higher” partner remained very small.

Geographical restrictions still held back hypergamy. Therefore, most men continued to have the opportunity to have sexual relations and start families. But then social networks, the iPhone and dating apps appeared.

## What social networks and apps have changed

This is where hypergamy went into overdrive: it was finally unleashed. Women now have access, in effect, to any man on the planet with a smartphone. And these apps want to keep you using them for as long as possible. Dating apps earn money from subscriptions; social media earns money from advertising. So the algorithms keep showing women the men who make them want to stay. Probably the most attractive ones, right? Or at least the ones who know how to present themselves well.

Because of this, women's demands are inflated to incredible proportions, and most men are recorded as unattractive. But that's not all. All of these apps—including Instagram, which has actually become the largest dating app in the world—force women to choose a partner based on a male model: purely by visual impression, by appearance.

Therefore, men who do not make an immediate external impression are immediately discarded. But women are not adapted to this method of choosing a partner. If you look at all of human history before the advent of social media, courtship was most often a slow, deliberate process. Especially for women: after all, they have to bear a child.

Nine months of pregnancy is a serious investment of time and energy, so women are naturally more selective than men. A woman would spend time getting to know a man properly. Can he lead? Can he stand up for himself? Does he want a serious relationship with me? Can he protect and provide for me? Who does he spend time with? Where does he stand among his peers? Does he have connections? Is he respected? She learned a lot of this from people in her social circle.

Now this leisurely process of getting to know each other and evaluating has almost disappeared. Women choose a man based on how good his Instagram is, how handsome he is, or how tall he is. And for most guys, the best photo is a fishing shot of them holding their best catch.

As a result, completely worthy men, who seem to have everything in order, do not even get a chance: women weed them out too quickly. And all this already affects society as a whole. Algorithms create what I call the illusion of choice: a woman thinks she has many great options when in fact she doesn’t.

In any case, if we are talking about a long, serious relationship. It may be easy for her to have sex with an attractive man. But getting him to connect his life with her is a completely different matter. After all, there are many women around him who are ready to sleep with him without obligation. If he is chosen by many, he has little incentive to settle on one.

But the woman will still sleep with him: after all, she has contraception, and abortion is also prohibited. ## Card game metaphor and fertility forecast

Essentially, women are playing blackjack with their reproductive years. If you do not know the rules, it is a card game where you try to get as close to 21 points as possible without going over. Suppose a woman in her twenties is dealt 18 points. That is not a bad hand.

This could be a promising guy who is already quite confident on his feet and wants a serious relationship with her. But she says: “No, I can find better.” And asks for another card.

Now she has 19 points. Then, let's say, 19 or 20. And she thinks: “Great, now I have a better man.”

By now, she is approaching thirty. And she thinks, “Ah, screw it. I can get to twenty-one. Hit me!”

And she busts. Over 21. Now she is about thirty-five, her childbearing years are running out, and she still has not found a man willing to commit. That is why our fertility rate is going down the toilet.

The graph shows another decline after social media took off in 2011. In America and other Western countries, fertility rates are between 1.4 and 1.6 children per woman, well below the 2.1 needed simply to replace the population. To give you an idea of how bad that is: in three generations, just about 75 years, the new generation will be only 30–40% of your population. Put simply, in a little over a hundred years we will all die out, and Africa will take our place, because its population triples with every generation.

This is where the current dating landscape is taking us. Hypergamy is running at full throttle. It has been unleashed. Every restraint has been removed. All the historical social constraints that accounted for our biology are gone.

As a result, dating is ruined for both men and women. And the population is simultaneously declining. But having said all this, I don't want anyone in the audience to start getting angry at women. They are not the only ones to blame. Now I will explain why.

## Environment, attention and personal responsibility

As I said, hypergamy is a natural instinct. Being angry at a woman for it is like being angry at her for feeling hungry. The problem is that a natural instinct has been placed in an unnatural environment: the global dating market.

We also have a natural craving for sugar and fat: we need them for survival. Or at least, in the conditions in which we evolved, they greatly helped to survive.

Now this instinct has found itself in an unnatural environment: there is an abundance of salt, fat and sugar around, because we produce a huge amount of industrially processed food. Because of this, people suffer from obesity and diabetes, and their body becomes less sensitive to insulin. Likewise, women receive so much attention from social networks that they develop what I call “attention diabetes”: familiar impressions no longer evoke the same response.

A compliment from an ordinary guy in a nearby coffee shop is no longer enough for her: free attention is already pouring into her from the Internet. Therefore, you need to be a truly outstanding man to break through this background and interest an ordinary woman. Of course, women have free will and the ability to make their own decisions.

They might limit social media and dating apps, just like a fat guy might decide he's done eating so much damn cake. Women can use the prefrontal cortex, an area of ​​the brain associated with planning and self-control, to make smarter decisions and curb instinctual impulses. But the problem is that women’s ability to make rational decisions is also destroyed.

Short videos have been shown to not only impair long-term attention spans, but also undermine decision-making and impulse control. Contraception also harms rational decision-making. It turns out that this ability of women is being attacked from both sides at once.

Given all this, I do not think it is fair to put all the blame on women. Just as it is unfair to blame the entire obesity epidemic on a lack of willpower. In modern society, especially in America, staying slim takes considerable discipline and persistence, as well as knowledge about nutrition that nobody is going to hand you on a plate.

And again: I’m not saying that fat people and women shouldn’t be responsible for their own decisions. Just remember that there are many external factors that put a spoke in the wheel and make everything much more difficult.

Ultimately, we live in a world for which our brains are not evolutionarily adapted. Both the basic female instinct of hypergamy and the ability to reason sensibly were under someone else’s control. It is modern technology that has driven us into this shitty situation.
